**Figure 1. Task schematic.** Participants choose between two options (A and B) on each click. Each option has a hidden latent value that depletes upon selection and recovers over time. Reward probability equals the latent value at the moment of the click, sampled as a Bernoulli outcome. The task consists of four hidden phases (90 s each): (1) symmetric baseline, (2) A-advantaged, (3) B-advantaged, and (4) symmetric scarcity with alternating 3-s bonus pulses. Phase transitions are not signaled to the participant.
