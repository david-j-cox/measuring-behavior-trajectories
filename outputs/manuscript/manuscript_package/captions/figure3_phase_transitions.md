**Figure 3. Behavioral dynamics around phase transitions.** Mean rolling choice proportion (top), switch rate (middle), and reward rate (bottom) aligned to the onset of each hidden phase change. Shaded regions show bootstrap 95% confidence bands (N = 60 sessions). Time zero marks the transition. Participants gradually reallocate behavior toward the newly advantageous option. Median adaptation lag at Phase 2 onset was 6.2 s (n = 60/60 adapted).
